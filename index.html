hyperframes — Declarative HTML/CSS/SVG to 60 FPS 4K Video Rendering Engine

hyperframes lets developers author motion graphics, data visualizations, and product videos using standard HTML, CSS, and SVG timelines. It captures deterministic frame-by-frame browser renders and pipes them into hardware-accelerated MP4/WebM encoders for jitter-free 60 FPS video production.

$ npx hyperframes render
